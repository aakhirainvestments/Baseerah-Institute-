High Impact Giving

Sponsor a student's
path to knowledge.

Some sisters want nothing more than to learn — but financial constraints stand in the way. Through our student sponsorship programme, your donation directly funds a sister's access to a course she otherwise couldn't attend.

Sponsorships can be full (covering all fees) or partial (covering a portion). Both make a real difference.

  • Full year Arabic Mastery — R300 registration + R800/month
  • Full year Hifth Programme — R300 registration + R900/month
  • Take a Leap to Speak — R750 once-off
  • 4-Week Aqīdah Course — R350 once-off

"Whoever guides someone to goodness will have a reward like one who did it." — Rasulullah ﷺ
