Who We Are

Rooted in tradition.
Open to all.

Baseerah Institute is a female-led Islamic institution, founded in 2016 by Shaykhah Rukayya Samsodien. We exist to raise the level of female scholarship in our community — through the teaching and publishing of traditional Islamic sciences, in an atmosphere of sisterhood and genuine academic excellence.

No prior knowledge of Islamic studies is required for any of our courses. Whether you are memorising the Qur'ān for the first time, learning Arabic from scratch, or deepening knowledge you have carried for years — there is always a place for you here.

Our Teacher

Shaykhah Rukayya
Samsodien

Shaykhah Rukayya completed memorisation of the Qur'ān under the late Shaykh Mohammed Yusuf Booley. She then spent six years studying in Damascus, Syria — a diploma from Abū Nour Institute, a degree in Sharī'ah from Al-Fath University, and an ijāza in the recitation of Hafs.

She served seven years as Principal and Hifth Consultant at Jam'Eyyatul Qurrā' Female Hifth Institute, and lectured full-time at Dārun Na'īm, Wynberg for six years. She is also an accredited Arabic–English translator of the High Court of South Africa.

Publications

  • Hifth Guide: An Educator's Manual
  • Al-Fiqh Al-Manhajī: The Book of Purification
