Our Story

Rooted in tradition.
Open to all.

Baseerah Institute was founded in 2016 out of a conviction that every woman deserves access to high-quality Islamic scholarship — in her own language, in her own city, led by women who have walked the path.

Who We Are

Female-led scholarship,
since 2016.

Baseerah Institute is a female-led Islamic institution that seeks to raise the level of female education and scholarship through the teaching and publishing of traditional Islamic texts and sciences. We offer part-time courses, short courses, and workshops throughout the year.

Founded by Shaykhah Rukayya Samsodien in response to a real gap she witnessed in Cape Town — accessible, rigorous, women-led Islamic education — Baseerah has grown from a small weekly gathering into a trusted institution serving over 2500 students.

We take pride in our holistic approach: fostering an atmosphere of sisterhood alongside academic excellence. Our classes are interactive. Our community is warm. And the pursuit of knowledge here is understood as a collective act of worship.

What We Stand For

The values we build on

📚

Traditional Scholarship

We teach from authentic, well-known primer texts in the fundamental Islamic sciences — not reformulated or diluted. The classics, made accessible.

🤝

Sisterhood First

Every class is a halaqah as much as a lesson. We cultivate togetherness, mutual encouragement, and a safe space where women grow together.

🌱

No Prerequisites

No prior Islamic studies required for any course. We meet students exactly where they are — whether complete beginners or those deepening existing knowledge.

🎓

Academic Excellence

We hold ourselves to a high standard — structured curricula, qualified teachers, and a commitment to genuine understanding, not surface-level familiarity.

💬

Interactive Learning

Questions are welcomed — expected, even. Our classes thrive on contribution, discussion, and the kind of engaged curiosity that makes knowledge stick.

🌍

Community Rooted

We exist to support the Cape Town Muslim community — complementing existing madāris and filling genuine gaps in female Islamic education.

Founder & Director

Shaykhah Rukayya
Samsodien

Shaykhah Rukayya completed memorisation of the Qur'ān under the late Shaykh Mohammed Yusuf Booley. She then returned to school and completed matric at Islamia College, after which she commenced six years of study in Damascus, Syria.

She received a diploma in Arabic Language and Islamic Studies from Abū Nour Institute, continued at Al-Fath University in the Faculty of Sharī'ah, and holds a degree in Sharī'ah. During her time in Syria, she recited the entire Qur'ān to Shaykh Abū al-Hasan Muhayyiddīn al-Kurdī in the narration of Hafs via the Ṭāriq of al-Shāṭibiyyah and received her sanad and ijāza therein.

She served seven years as Principal and Hifth Consultant at Jam'Eyyatul Qurrā' Female Hifth Institute, and lectured full-time at Dārun Na'īm, Wynberg for six years. She is also an accredited Arabic–English translator of the High Court of South Africa.
