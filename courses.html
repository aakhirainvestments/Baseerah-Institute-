Arabic Mastery Programme

Flagship R800/month In-person + Online
+

About this programme

The first of its kind in Cape Town — a comprehensive two-year Arabic journey designed to give you a firm grasp of the Arabic language and its workings. We graduate students who can read, understand, and engage with the Qur'ān and classical texts with real confidence.

Subjects covered

  • Nahw & I'rab — Grammar, context of words, the art of translation
  • Sarf — Morphology, creating words, mastering verbs and their meanings
  • Arabic Literature — Qur'ān, Hadith, classical & modern texts, traditional poetry
  • Conversational Arabic — Based on the Leap to Speak series

Prerequisites

The ability to read basic Arabic text, or a strong desire to learn and improve these skills.

Part-Time · Hifth

Ladies Hifth Programme

4 Streams available In-person only
+

About this programme

Pioneering an innovative Hifth structure designed for busy moms, mature students, and professionals. Students choose from one of four streams based on their memorisation goal.

Four Streams

  • Two Juz — Memorise Juz 29 & 30
  • Oft-Recited Suwar — Mulk, Yasin, Sajdah, Waqi'ah, Rahman, Kahf
  • Five Suwar — Juz 29, 30, 1, 2, 3
  • Full Qur'ān — Complete 30 Juz

Method of instruction

  • Designated time in class to prepare Sabaq / New Lessons
  • Daily Nasihah + Tajwīd from the Murshid al-Qari

Prerequisites

None — except a desire to learn the Book of Allah. Targeted at students aged 20+.

Thursday Arabic Dawrah — The Sealed Nectar

Ladies only Online + In-person

About this gathering

A weekly ladies-only gathering studying Al-Rahīq Al-Makhtūm (The Sealed Nectar) — awarded first prize out of 171 manuscripts by the Muslim World League in 1979. Accompanied by selected excerpts from Fiqh al-Sīrah by Shaykh Ramadān al-Būtī.

Course objectives

  • Deepen your love for Rasulullah ﷺ
  • Learn about his remarkable life, teachings, and character
  • Embody his noble traits and qualities in daily life

Take a Leap to Speak — Conversational Arabic

R750 Book included Trusted by 2500+
+

About this course

A practical, conversation-based Arabic course designed especially for women, focused on helping you actually speak Arabic in your everyday life — from Lesson 1. Facilitated by Shaykhah Rukayya Samsodien.

What you'll learn

  • Introduce yourself with confidence
  • Ask & answer everyday questions
  • Name fruits, groceries, describe your home
  • Masculine & feminine words in context
  • Real conversations from the very first session

Why it matters

Imagine visiting the Holy Lands and actually speaking to people. Welcoming an Arab sister into your home in her own language. This course makes that possible.
